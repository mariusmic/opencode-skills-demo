# Bake a Simple Vanilla Cake

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour an 8-inch cake pan.
2. Gather:
   - 1½ cups all-purpose flour
   - 1 cup sugar
   - 1½ teaspoons baking powder
   - ½ cup softened butter
   - 2 eggs
   - ½ cup milk
   - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
3. Whisk the flour and baking powder in a bowl.
4. In another bowl, beat the butter and sugar until fluffy. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
5. Mix in the dry ingredients, alternating with the milk. Stop when just combined.
6. Pour into the pan and bake for 30–35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Cool for 10 minutes before turning the cake out. Let it cool fully before frosting.
