# Make a Simple Homemade Pizza

1. Heat the oven to 475°F (245°C). Preheat a pizza stone if using one.
2. Gather:
   - 1 pound pizza dough
   - ½ cup pizza sauce
   - 8 ounces shredded mozzarella
   - Desired toppings
   - A little flour or cornmeal
3. Stretch the dough on a lightly floured surface into a 12-inch round.
4. Transfer it to a baking sheet or preheated stone.
5. Spread on the sauce, leaving a small border. Add cheese and toppings.
6. Bake for 10–15 minutes, until the crust is browned and the cheese bubbles.
7. Rest for a few minutes, slice, and serve.
